## 2026-10-18 — the corpus brings its own stop list

Don's named limit («STOP list stays English, other languages run a noisier hebbian») was under-named: English STOP plus the `length>2` gate let «что», «это», «как», «для» through `isContent`, so a Russian field put stop-words into the hebbian window and *waited for them as witnesses* — the field expected «это» and called it fulfilled. Fix, one block in `buildField`: `CSTOP` — the corpus's own short tokens (3–4 letters), most frequent first, until they cover the first 1% of the text, and never fewer than 8 when the corpus is small; `isContent` checks it beside STOP. Any alphabet brings its stop list with itself; nothing is hardcoded. Proofs, headless probe (`.probe/chain.js`, stub DOM, real engine) on this machine (neo, node 24): embedded Q corpus, 80,885 tokens — CSTOP is just «the» (the rest was already in STOP), chain closes, origin seams 4.92 / 5.04; «Камень и воздух» (Mandelstam essay, 2,305 tokens, 152 sentences) — CSTOP «что это как она его уже есть был», chain closes, debt 0.282, 7 cracks, backward reads «но камень отнят немоты так, что слово — это камень»; before the ≥8 floor «это» was still being expected and fulfilled on this corpus; Hebrew essay «השורש כזיכרון» — tokenizes and closes a 12-step chain (debt 0.256), RTL layout on the tape still unchecked.
