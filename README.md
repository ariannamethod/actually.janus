# actually.janus

a door that time passes through in both directions. not an arrow drawn backward over a forward engine — the engine itself walks both ways.

## the claim

every Janus before this one wore its past as a costume. janus-bpe raised temperature and called it backward. nanojanus flipped a direction coefficient inside an overlay. Q swapped a destiny-guided prompt for a random one. all three generate left to right and draw the arrow wherever they like, and the family's own audit said it plainly: tokens are not reversed. two independent readers hit that same line in one night. this organism exists because of that sentence.

here backward is a mechanism. the field reads its corpus twice — what follows a pair, and what precedes one — and a backward sentence grows from the seam inward: its last word is chosen first, because that word has to meet the beginning of what already happened. the chain to the left of the slit is a chain of causes, each one picked for the sake of its consequence.

## what lives in the file

one html file, the whole organism. a tape of sentences lying horizontal, passing through a slit at screen centre; the origin sits in the slit and both streams move through it. the field: forward and reverse bigrams, trigrams and a hebbian window, rebuilt from any .txt you drop on the page, any alphabet that has letters. expectations: before each step the field names the word it waits for; a witness either fills the hollow mark or cracks it, and the crack stays — debt is 0.9·debt + 0.1·gap, visible as relief before you read a single word. rereading: after every step all seams are rescored and the past is relabeled evidence or contradiction, with hysteresis so it doesn't flicker. weak seams are regrown and the old sentence stays beneath the new one as a layer — ten minutes in, the tape is geology. two calendars (365.25 and 354) drift under the tape as moiré; the file remembers the day you first opened it. your own words enter the metaweights, in both directions.

## build & run

```
open janus.html
```

or serve it with any static server. no dependencies, no build step, no weights. drop any .txt of 200+ characters to replace the corpus.

## verified

live browser on this exact file, 2026-10-18: a 12-step chain closes; backward seam 4.83 on the embedded Q corpus; the same door fed Russian — 1,068 tokens, 120 sentences, both direction tables built — grows a Russian past and a Russian future around «дверь помнит всех, кто прошёл сквозь неё», chain closed, debt 0.158, one crack left open. the log of changes with their proofs is [TRUELOG.md](TRUELOG.md).

## not yet

weights are not in the file; the header says FIELD · NO WEIGHTS and means it. the second layer is designed and waiting: haiku1/haiku2 as two faces, the backward face judged by retrodiction through the seam — which past best explains the present it claims to have caused. RTL alphabets pass the tokenizer, but how the tape lies for them is unchecked. the corpus brings its own stop list (its most frequent short tokens), so other alphabets keep stop-words out of the hebbian and out of expectations. these are statuses, not apologies.
