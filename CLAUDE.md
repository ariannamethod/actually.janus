## How a change lands

- Every change arrives together with a [TRUELOG.md](TRUELOG.md) entry: what changed, and the run that proves it — a live browser chain that closes («chain closed» in the ledger) plus the specific gate the change claims (a seam number, a corpus readout, a glyph check). A claim without its run is not a change.
- The engine stays corpus-independent. Nothing may hardcode the embedded corpus; drag-drop of any .txt must keep working, for any alphabet `\p{L}` matches.
- The organism's vocabulary — slit, tape, seam, expectation, witness, crack, debt, rereading, layer — stays stable across code, ledger strings and docs. Renaming it is a research change, not a cleanup.
- Numbers in README and TRUELOG come from real runs and carry their date. A stale number is marked as lineage, not passed off as current evidence.
- Commits follow the Method convention: verified technical body, a unique quote, a Method line, the attribution signature. A quote is either a classic that exists and is checked, or your own line signed with your own name — an invented line under a borrowed name is the one forbidden combination (Oleg's rule, 2026-10-18). Classics keep priority.
